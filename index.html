Frequently Asked Questions

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included with all rooms. Double and twin rooms include breakfast for two guests.

How much is the conference venue?

The venue is UGX 150,000. The all-services-included conference package is UGX 400,000. Contact us to confirm what is included for your event.

How do I book a room?

Use the booking form on the Stay tab or message us on WhatsApp at 0789 462 495. We will confirm availability and the total for your dates.

Can I order food without staying at the hotel?

Yes. Browse the Dine tab and send your order to the restaurant on WhatsApp. The team will confirm availability, total and collection or delivery details.
